# Title
The problem as the customer would say it, for example "My website shows 502 Bad Gateway".

## Symptom
What the customer sees: the exact error message, on which page or service, and since when.

## Quick check
One or two commands that confirm the problem in under a minute.

## Diagnosis
The steps in order, most likely cause first. For each step: the command, what a healthy result looks like, and what a broken result looks like.

## Fix
The correct fix and why it works. Also the tempting wrong fix, and why I should avoid it.

## Verify
How I prove it is fixed: one check on the server, and one check as the customer would see it.

## Prevention
What stops this from happening again: monitoring, an alert, or a configuration change.

## Escalate when
What I would see that means this is a different problem, so I should stop and escalate.
